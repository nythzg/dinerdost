## Operator paths

- **Overview:** observed metrics, adoption rules and measured local health. Enter actual acquisition expenses under Plans & billing to enable CAC inputs.
- **Restaurants:** onboarding defaults, apply templates, brand reporting grants, branding and logo uploads. Assigned viewers use `/enterprise`; grants never confer ordinary cross-tenant owner API access.
- **Plans & billing:** configure invoice issuer and recipient, then enable a recorded subscription lifecycle. Billing anchors become fixed after invoicing. Inspect immutable documents, record actual receipts or correcting credits, activate future fee rules and record actual held funds/settlements.
- **Dining & POS:** assign master menu tags and manage QR lifecycle. Retiring an occupied table is rejected; rotation warns that an old printed QR stops working. Existing table/order/visit identities remain intact.
- **Internal team:** explicitly select permitted restaurants for restricted staff. SUPERADMIN stays global; the last-superadmin safeguard and password step-up remain.
- **Support & broadcasts:** open the existing password-confirmed support grant, then **Open owner workspace**. Its separate cookie grants at most the grant's remaining 15 minutes. PostgreSQL READ ONLY and tenant RLS enforce every owner service transaction; mutation HTTP requests are rejected. **Exit owner view** preserves the original platform/restaurant sign-ins. Configure support deadlines, attach evidence and resolve internal tasks here.

Historical manual SaaS ledger entries and promotion credits remain separate from newly generated document/receipt records; they are not silently imported as actual collections or automatic deductions. Use an explicitly reviewed document credit to correct a generated invoice. No historical balance is rewritten or inferred.

Every change needs a reason and retains optimistic versions, exact retries and fresh permission checks. Financial mutations serialize against the platform audit head and lock the affected records. SaaS documents, receipts, contract history, settlement entries, fee accruals/corrections, attachments and QR events retain immutable history. Diner prices, invoices, original payments, stock costs/FIFO and physical counts are separate.
