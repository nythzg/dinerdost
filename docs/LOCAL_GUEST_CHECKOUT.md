# Guest cart, saved charges and tracking - 24 October 2026

Local web implementation. No commit, push or deployment.

## Current behavior

- The guest has a dedicated cart reached from the floating Order/View cart controls. The course trail and floating bar disappear in that view. Dessert no longer contains an embedded cart. The cart contains item quantities, customer details, coupon entry, a mutually exclusive loyalty claim, named tax percentages, itemized charges, rounding and the final total.
- The server prices previews from current menu/variant/modifier records and the bill's recorded tax settings. Previewing neither creates an order nor reserves rewards. Submission rechecks the displayed payable, identity, visit, item availability, reward eligibility and capacity in the existing audited/idempotent transaction; failures roll back the order and reward changes together.
- Guest loyalty remains limited to an identity recognized on that device or its active table order. An unrelated device entering another customer's name/phone cannot see their balance or claim it. The optional local question about broadening this policy remains unanswered. No phone verification or OTP step was reintroduced, and stored verification facts are unchanged.
- POS retains its existing owner tax editor, with explicit rates and the existing future-orders-only persistence. Future bill settings opens the shared adjustments form: coupon definitions, fixed charges, loyalty cap and rounding save together under an outlet version guard. Existing manager permissions for coupons/cap/rounding remain; only the owner can change saved charges, matching the existing charge/tax permission boundary.
- Fixed rupee charges apply once per new web bill, on its first round, for staff POS, guest QR and storefront orders. Later rounds do not add them again. They use the existing separately itemized other-charge treatment. Charges and rounding are snapshotted; changing future defaults cannot retrofit current bills, issued invoices, payments, costs or stock. Native application behavior is unchanged.
- Tracking retains real kitchen progress and now shows table/date/customer name, quantity and recorded item prices, coupon or loyalty name, named taxes and percentages, charges, rounding, received and outstanding amounts. It reads order/round snapshots, not current outlet defaults. Guest tracking does not expose customer phone numbers.
