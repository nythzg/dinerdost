## Master acceptance criteria

- **AC01, QR optional:** automated checks cover QR disabled while staff ordering works, code-free visits, legacy single-use pairing, device isolation and grant revocation.
- **AC02, bill isolation:** automated checks cover provisional documents without payment/KOT transition, blocked premature final settlement and reprint flags. Physical print interception is unverified.
- **AC03, identity:** tested before staff payment and zero-total authorization; customer QR captures identity before submission. Kiosk, D2C and EDC channels are not built.
- **AC04, loyalty:** reserve/lock/earn lifecycle and expired-token protection are tested. External notification delivery and OTP are absent.
- **AC05, forensic controls:** tenant/role filtering, immutable rows and chain verification are tested. Native biometric approval and signed offline trails are absent.
- **AC06, LAN failover:** not implemented or tested.

This release provides a working web core for review and development. It is not a declaration that the full PRD or live restaurant rollout has passed acceptance.

The separate [client-feedback checklist](CLIENT_FEEDBACK.md) contains 56 scoped requests: 52 implemented and four user-deferred live integrations. Version 0.3.5 is deployed after local, isolated Neon and production-browser verification. Requirement-labelled buttons are implemented for deferred integrations; they do not claim operational messaging, fingerprint attendance or aggregator feeds. The confirmed WhatsApp trigger is order-token issuance. Android preparation is tracked separately from the web release.
